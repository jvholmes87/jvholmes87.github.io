How I work

I spent 15 years in USAF civil engineering, managing geospatial records, as-built drawings and a drone inspection program. Now I do quality control and project coordination on federal construction work in South Korea, and am pursuing an MS in Information Technology with a Systems Engineering concentration at UMGC.

I build with AI-assisted coding. I set the requirements, make and log the decisions, run the tests and playtests, and approve every release. The AI implements under that control. Forkline is the public proof of that method.
