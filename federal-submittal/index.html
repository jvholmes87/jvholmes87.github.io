03 / explore a dependency

What if access arrives after the deadline?

A site-dependent submittal can be due before its prerequisite is available. Move the access day to see the timing relationship. This synthetic example assumes a deadline of award + 30 calendar days and zero preparation time after access.

A negative margin identifies a conflict under those assumptions. A nonnegative margin does not establish practical feasibility, contractual compliance or an approved remedy.

04 / measurement discipline

Keep the denominator attached.

The 11 September 2026 snapshot reports 20/29 first-pass approvals by dispositioned line item and 8/17 by package. A package can contain several line items. The switcher above changes the analytical unit, not the time period.

The dashboard's 85% current-state gate share answers a different question. Superseded and earlier gate-row percentages are not combined into an improvement claim.
