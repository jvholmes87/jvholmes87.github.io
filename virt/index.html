01 / the working prototype

Make a growing campaign legible.

HolmesNet began as a documented playthrough of Bobiverse: Replicant Too. VIRT gives its dated records a spatial interface: a nearby-star atlas, system details and console-specific workspaces.

I set the direction, review the interfaces and visual work, and use AI-assisted development to iterate. The longer-term engineering application is a development direction.

02 / one interface, several questions

From map to operational context.

01

Where?

Three.js and the HYG star catalog provide astronomical context. J2000.0 coordinates are not propagated to the campaign year.

02

What is known?

System inventory, assignments and freight retain their own checkpoint dates. Unknown vessel coordinates stay unknown.

03

What comes next?

Analysis separates observations from deferred destinations and constraints. Planned missions are not shown as dispatched.

03 / verification with boundaries

Inspect the experience, then inspect the evidence.

In the 17 November review, the atlas rendered, selecting Sol updated its record, and Analysis displayed observed, planned and unknown information.

The development log records 31 site tests and my October 31 Android acceptance of GUPPI Refinement 07. Those tests and device checks were not rerun in this portfolio review. Neither establishes a measured frame rate, VR acceptance or final character likeness.

Prototype boundary

  • Dated campaign snapshots
  • No live game synchronization
  • No autonomous orders or real-world control
  • Expression, voice and further character refinement remain future work
